# 60-second walkthrough script

0–10s: “This is EnergyPilot: turning energy readings into explainable waste alerts. This office and all readings are synthetic.” Show the overnight story and key metrics.

10–25s: Point to orange chart bars. Open Investigation. “The recent same-hour median is the baseline. These hours exceed twice that baseline by at least one kWh. This suggests an investigation; it does not diagnose faulty equipment.”

25–40s: Show evidence rows. “I would check occupancy, HVAC timers and persistent loads.” Change tariff to €0.30 and avoidable share to 50%. Explain that the estimate covers only observed excess.

40–50s: Export and open the report. Show the assumptions and supporting readings.

50–60s: Open Data & imports; import `data/invalid-example.csv`. Show clear rejection reasons. Re-load demo and show 384 duplicates skipped. “A database key prevents duplicate readings. This prototype supports one building and excludes DST transition datasets.”

Recording: use your screen recorder on the running local app or deployed demo. This file is a script, not a recorded video.
